The product

One workspace from data to decision

Route Studio

Live map of real stops, per-stop lateness risk, schedule bands of simulated arrivals against windows, and a decision panel with paired CIs and the candidate frontier explaining why a plan was chosen.

Disruption Lab

Place a traffic area or urgent order on the map, break a van, inject: three policies re-plan in seconds; reassigned stops and the measured outcome are shown.

Evidence

Every experiment read from stored artifacts: forest plots, target control, misspecification, dynamic factorial, model calibration.

Data and Method

Provenance on every screen: observed, model estimate, synthetic, algorithm output, measured.
