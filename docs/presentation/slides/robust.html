Real deployments must calibrate variability on telematics data

Higher variability raises both the gain and the cost: KAIROS +4.9 / +9.7 / +11.9 pt over baseline at low / medium / high

Two honest messages. The target slider has a real, monotone effect on the plan and its cost. But attainment depends on the planner’s variability model: underestimating dispersion by half drops target attainment to 58%. Overestimating also hurts slightly, because no candidate qualifies under the inflated model and the fallback picks the most reliable plan under the wrong model. This is the main thing to fix with real data.
