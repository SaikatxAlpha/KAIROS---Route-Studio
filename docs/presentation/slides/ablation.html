Buffers alone over-protect and, at 99%, make windows infeasible so the plan gets worse.

At 95% the Amazon baseline already meets the target: KAIROS correctly adds nothing.

At 99%, KAIROS lifts on-time to 99.7% [+1.3, +2.2 pt].

Each cell: Δ on-time (percentage points) · Δ travel cost. Amazon: 24 merged station-days from held-out dates; cost = driving time + dispatch

The ablation is the strongest evidence for the design. Buffering blindly is expensive and can backfire; selecting among unbuffered plans has nothing safer to pick. The combination meets the target at the lowest cost, and does nothing when nothing is needed, as on Amazon at 95%, whose synthetic promise windows are lenient.
