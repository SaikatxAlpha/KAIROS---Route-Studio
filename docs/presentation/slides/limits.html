Limitations

What the evidence does not show

  • Gains are measured under a synthetic variability model, not real operations
  • On-time rate is an expected share, not a route-level guarantee
  • Amazon test dates cover two cities; map lines are straight segments
  • Disruption sizes are illustrative and known once they occur
  • DN-OR-01 statement unpublished at build time

Next steps and impact

From benchmark to depot

  • Calibrate the scenario model on telematics arrival logs; the conformal pipeline carries over
  • Learn service-time distributions per stop type
  • Price the cost of reliability for a depot manager: minutes late saved per extra driving hour
  • Add learned driver preferences once the core is validated
Be explicit about what we cannot claim. The method is ready for real data: the same planner, simulator and conformal calibration would run on per-stop timestamps. The decision support value is making the reliability and cost trade-off visible and controllable.
