Traffic, long doorstep stops, a broken van or an urgent order turn a tight plan into late deliveries. Conventional planners optimise fixed inputs and never see the risk.

Measured: deterministic PyVRP plans, 500 synthetic scenarios per instance, medium variability

Start with the gap. The deterministic baseline meets every window on its own expected times. Under our documented variability model it falls to 91.8% of stops on time on average, and only 39% of the 56 instances reach 95%. The variability is synthetic (no public dataset records realised trip times), which we say on every screen.
