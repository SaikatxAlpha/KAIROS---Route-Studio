Research question

Can uncertainty-aware, dynamically re-optimised routes reduce delivery-window violations under controlled disruptions, compared with a deterministic baseline, at acceptable cost in distance and compute?

Treated as a hypothesis: paired experiments, held-out scenarios, ablations, failed and null results reported.

We framed this as a test, not a claim. The answer we found is nuanced: yes on tight time windows and at high reliability targets, no measurable effect where the baseline already meets the target, and a hard dependence on the uncertainty model being right.
