A full factorial separates the two contributions. On Solomon’s tight windows, starting from a reliability-aware plan matters more than how you repair it. On Amazon, whose promise windows are lenient, the static KAIROS plan at 95% is essentially the baseline, and what matters is re-planning at all; deterministic re-optimisation is nearly as good and three times faster. We report that as it is.
